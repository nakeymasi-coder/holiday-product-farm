Where each product gets sold

Platform findings from the report — what each channel is actually doing this holiday.

TikTok Shop

  • Surprise #1: Custom wrapping paper was the #1-performing product across Printify merchant stores on TikTok Shop last holiday — ahead of hoodies. Then: Gildan 5000 tee (#2), blank wall calendars (#3 — and calendars sell into January), Comfort Colors 1717 tee (#4).
  • 26% of TikTok users bought or planned to buy gifts found on TikTok last holiday. No minimum follower count to sell.
  • Converting formats: gift-haul videos, cozy morning-routine videos (sweatshirts/blankets), gift-wrap/unboxing reveals. Hashtags pushed: #tiktokshopchristmas, #christmasgiftideas, #secretsanta.
  • The audience skews younger than women 40+ — use TikTok for discovery and gift-buyer reach (adult children buying for mom/grandma), not as the only storefront. Source: Printify TikTok Shop Playbook, Sep 29, 2026

Etsy + the rest

  • Etsy: Q4 is 40–60% of annual revenue for many sellers (secondary guide estimate). Winning Q4 searches: “custom ornament,” “personalized stocking,” “secret Santa gift.” Personalized friend gifts +1,400%; pet gifting searches doubled (Etsy Fall/Winter 2026 Trend Edit).
  • Etsy 2026 aesthetics (eRank): Heirloom Holidays, Classic Heritage (tartan, velvet, dark wood), Glamorous Entertaining (Art Deco, velvet, navy/plum/black + gold), Blue & Silver celestial, Enchanted Winter Garden.
  • Gen-AI photo effects are not allowed on Etsy (Teeinblue policy note) — use AI-restyled art on your own storefront/TikTok, not Etsy.
  • Amazon Merch: No official 2026 holiday trend report found. Guide consensus: Q4 spikes income, but it's invite-only with slow tiers (start Tier 10) and ~$2.21–$4.00 royalty on a $19.99 tee — not a primary channel for an Oct 28 pop-up launch unless an account/tier already exists.
  • Printify / Printful guides agree: apparel leads; build around proven categories + personalization; merchandize by recipient (family = matching tees, ornaments, blankets, mugs, cards; friends = sweatshirts, mugs, stickers, totes, candles).

🛡️ Stay safe — the trademark minefield

“Christmas is a trademark minefield, and it's the season when takedowns spike” (Trendlytic). A December takedown can cost the season's payout. Check every phrase in USPTO TESS (Live status, Class 25 apparel / Class 28 ornaments) before designing.

🚫 Do NOT use

  • Grinch / How the Grinch Stole Christmas — Dr. Seuss Enterprises, live reg. 4038720 / 4798685, expressly covering Christmas tree ornaments; also “Grinchmas” and derivatives, and the character likeness.
  • The Elf on the Shelf — live reg. 5020389 (CCA and B, LLC), incl. ornaments. Say “Christmas elf” generically instead.
  • Rudolph the Red-Nosed Reindeer — live reg. 1309723 + copyrighted song. A generic reindeer is fine; a red-nosed reindeer named Rudolph / song lyrics are not.
  • Frosty the Snowman — live reg. 2522936 (Warner Bros.) + copyrighted song. Generic snowman = fine.
  • Disney / Peanuts / Elf (film) / Nightmare Before Christmas / Hallmark Keepsake (live Hallmark reg. for ornaments) — characters, likenesses, titles, quotes.
  • Movie quotes/titles: “Merry Christmas Ya Filthy Animal” (Home Alone, registered since 2019), “A Christmas Story” as a title mark, Buddy-the-Elf quotes/images.
  • Copyrighted songs: “All I Want for Christmas Is You” (registered), “Santa Claus Is Comin' to Town” (registered + copyrighted), “Last Christmas,” “White Christmas,” “Winter Wonderland,” “Let It Snow,” “Jingle Bell Rock,” “Little Drummer Boy,” “Santa Baby” (avoid building a lead product on it).
  • Brand/team marks: NFL/NBA/university names & logos, Stanley/Starbucks-style trade dress on tumblers, designer monograms in “glam” patterns — glam must be original pattern-work, not knockoff logos.
  • Other sellers' artwork — including popular Black Santa / grandma illustrations; copyright applies even without a trademark. And read bundle licenses: several Etsy faith/coquette PNG bundles explicitly ban Amazon, TikTok, and Redbubble use.

✅ Generally safer ground

Still check USPTO per phrase / product class.

  • Your own original generic Santa Claus / Mrs. Claus drawing — the generic jolly Santa figure is public-domain folklore; specific Coca-Cola-style or artist-specific renderings are not yours to copy.
  • Generic reindeer / snowman / gingerbread / nutcracker motifs.
  • “Merry Christmas” and “Jesus is the reason for the season” as a common religious expression in your own original layout — widely used; the artwork/layout must be originated, and expect zero exclusivity.
  • Bible verses: the text of public-domain translations (e.g. KJV) is public domain; modern translations like NIV/ESV are copyrighted — quote KJV or paraphrase and verify.
  • Public-domain carols, original versions only: Silent Night, Deck the Halls, Jingle Bells, Joy to the World, O Holy Night, Away in a Manger, Hark! The Herald Angels Sing, The First Noel, We Three Kings — not modern arrangements, recordings, or later lyric translations.
Open questions — what the research could NOT verify
  • Printify & Gelato 2026 Christmas cutoffs — not published as of Oct 23, 2026; any ~Dec 10 date circulating is their 2025 date. Re-check Printify's Network Fulfillment Status page in early November.
  • Printful knitwear 2026 cutoff — not published; 2025's was Nov 15 and knit takes 8–14 business days. (Another reason: no knit sweaters this season.)
  • Plus-size–specific demand data — no 2026 trend report from Printful/Printify/Etsy quantifying plus-size holiday demand was found. The plus-size play rests on fit-range availability (S–5XL standard, 6XL specialty) and competitors under-serving it — not measured demand.
  • Amazon Merch official 2026 holiday trends — no first-party report found; Merch findings come from seller guides (Merch Informer, Prinil, Accio), lower confidence.
  • Bookish/cozy sales volumes — listing-level evidence only, no category sales figures found. That's why the bookish tote is a Wave-3 secondary bet, not the lead.
